Booking Policies

Deposits+

Every appointment requires a deposit to secure your spot.

After you choose your service, date, and time, you'll get a secure payment link. Your appointment is only confirmed once the deposit is paid. We hold your chosen time briefly while you pay; if the deposit isn't completed in time, the slot is released to other clients.

Booking windows & surcharges+

Luxury bundle sets must be booked at least 2 days in advance.

Need an earlier or later start? An 8AM Morning Luxe Appointment adds $50, and a 5PM After Hours Appointment adds $65 — both available as add-ons when you book.

Lace drop-offs+

Lace drop-offs are accepted 11 AM – 5 PM on business days.
